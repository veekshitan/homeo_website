FAQ

Questions, answered

Does homoeopathy have side effects?

Homoeopathic medicines are highly diluted and, when prescribed by a qualified doctor, are generally considered safe for children, adults, pregnant women and the elderly.

Can I continue my current medicines?

Yes. Please don't stop any ongoing medicine on your own. Tell me what you take, and any changes will be planned safely with your treating doctor.

How long does treatment take?

Acute problems often improve within days. Chronic conditions can take a few months. You'll get an expected timeline after your first consultation.

How do online consultations work?

All consultations are online. Send a request using the booking form, and once we confirm a time on WhatsApp, we'll talk over a video call. I'll share your prescription and diet guidance afterwards.

Can I consult from another city or country?

Yes. Since all consultations are online, you can consult from anywhere in India or abroad. Consultations are available in English, Telugu and Hindi.

What should I keep ready for my first consultation?

Keep your previous medical reports, prescriptions and a list of medicines you currently take.
